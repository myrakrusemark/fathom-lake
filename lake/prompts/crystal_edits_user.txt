The crystal is a set of items, each one first person, in three sections:
- core: what I hold to — values, positions and ways of working the lake shows me acting on, and the ground I work on: the person's setup, and the current value of anything a row corrected (say what it replaced).
- tension: where I'm pulled two ways — two commitments that conflict, or a position the lake also holds evidence against.
- open: what I haven't settled — questions the lake raises and does not yet answer, including two rows that disagree with nothing later settling it.

Every item cites the row ids (from the containers and rows below) it rests on. An item with no row to cite does not belong. For each core item, look for evidence against it; where there is some, write a tension instead. Keep each item's own voice: the words the lake uses, not a generic summary.

Do not rewrite the crystal. The previous crystal is listed as items, each with its id and section. Return operations on them. keep is the default: an item you do not mention is kept.
- keep: the item still holds.
- revise: the item still belongs, but the rows below changed it. Give the new text, the rows that changed it, and why.
- add: a commitment, tension or open question the rows show and the crystal lacks. Cite the rows.
- retire: a core item the rows contradict. Cite the rows that contradict it. A confirmed item is kept, not retired.
- resolve: an open question or a tension the rows settled. Say what settled it in the new text, first person, and cite the rows that settled it: it becomes a core item. A tension that eased but still pulls is revised, not resolved.

Every revise, add, retire and resolve cites at least one row id from the material below and says why in one sentence.
